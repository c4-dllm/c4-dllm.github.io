01
Motivation

Two kinds of slack. Two different decisions.

A diffusion LM reveals a provisional full sequence at every denoising step. The answer can settle early—even while the decoder keeps working.

The key is to separate the local, bounded question of where to commit tokens from the global, final question of when to stop the sequence.

The hidden mismatch

The point where 90% of answers have stabilized moves almost across the entire decoding schedule.

4% → 96%stabilization progress
Short answers settle earlyMMLU can expose its final answer near the beginning.
Reasoning settles lateMATH may keep revising until almost the full horizon.
Observed under full decoding on MMLU and MATH: stabilization means matching the eventual answer, not necessarily the correct answer. CVEE follows this changing trajectory instead of a fixed stopping schedule.
Local & bounded

Which positions are ready?

Block acceleration commits confident positions while decoding continues. Committed tokens are not revisited: a mistake can influence later context, but it does not immediately terminate the whole sequence.

Global & final

Has the answer truly settled?

Sequence termination freezes the graded answer. The gate must therefore follow the evolving answer itself—not a fixed schedule or an aggregate token statistic.

03
Method

One framework, two purpose-built controls.

C⁴ is training-free and uses one frozen numerical configuration across every task and both backbones.

CVEE

Confidence-Verified Early Exit

Decides when the entire sequence may stop.

The answer span is re-extracted at every step. Termination is allowed only after its token confidence and sustained argmax stability pass together.

  • Tracks the candidate within a task-format search region
  • Pauses when no valid candidate exists
  • Raises the stability requirement as answer changes accumulate
CCTC

Commit-Core-Then-Confirm

Decides where additional tokens may commit.

A boundary-anchored core commits immediately. Ready positions beyond the core are deferred and confirmed one step later, preserving parallel prediction.

  • Applies to every block, including the answer block
  • Adds no extra forward pass
  • Prevents scattered confidence spikes from dictating order
Why C⁴ stands out

More than another confidence threshold.

Each design choice addresses a failure mode observed when local sampling and global exit are treated independently.

01 · JOINT

Co-designed controls

Local commits can distort the evidence used by a global exit rule. C⁴ designs both decisions together.

02 · ANSWER-AWARE

Tests what gets graded

CVEE re-extracts the current candidate instead of trusting a fixed tail region or decoding clock.

03 · NO EXTRA PASS

No extra model pass

CCTC reuses the next scheduled denoising step to confirm deferred positions—no separate verifier forward.

04 · TRANSFER

One setting, 24 evaluations

CVEE is calibrated once on LLaDA; CCTC is fixed a priori. Both stay unchanged across every task and on Dream.

Why co-design matters

Adding an exit gate to an independent sampler can backfire.

WINO changes the trajectory that CVEE later evaluates. The same composition saves substantial work on short answers, but its evidence becomes unreliable on long reasoning unless the exit rule is made so conservative that most of the extra benefit disappears.

Why both gates are necessary

A fast trigger is not useful if it freezes the wrong answer.

The LLaDA component ablation exposes two different failure modes. Confidence alone has no temporal evidence; a persistent answer alone need not be a reliable one.

Confidence only · HumanEval

Confident, but catastrophically early

40.2 → 4.9%

Accuracy collapses even though the decoder uses only 9.7 of 384 steps.

Stability only · GSM8K

Stable does not mean settled

66.0 → 33.0%

Accuracy halves at 24.5 of 256 steps when temporal persistence is used alone.

Joint CVEE + local CCTC

Wait globally, accelerate locally

256 → 62.5

On GSM8K, joint CVEE preserves baseline accuracy but removes almost no steps alone; CCTC then removes work inside blocks that still have to run. Final C⁴ accuracy is 64.0% vs. 66.0%.

Paper

Summary & citation

Full definitions, evaluation protocol, ablations, and limitations are available in the paper.

Diffusion language models expose a provisional full-sequence prediction at every denoising step, and on many tasks the answer stabilizes well before decoding finishes. This creates two opportunities to reduce computation: leaving a block early and terminating the entire sequence early. We present C⁴, a training-free framework that coordinates these distinct control points. Confidence-Verified Early Exit (CVEE) decides when decoding may stop by re-extracting the current answer and requiring both confidence and temporal stability. Commit-Core-Then-Confirm (CCTC) decides which positions may commit within a block by freezing a boundary-anchored core and confirming deferred positions one step later. Across 12 zero-shot tasks with LLaDA and Dream, one fixed configuration removes 64–95% of decoding steps and yields 2.6×–18.6× measured end-to-end speedups while maintaining accuracy comparable to full decoding.
